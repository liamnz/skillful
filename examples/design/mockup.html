Customer

customer

Definition

A person or business that has created an account with the shop and accepted the terms of service, whether or not they have bought anything.

Discouraged terms

  • client — Finance and the old CRM say 'client'
  • shopper — Marketing copy

Examples

  • Someone who signed up yesterday and has not ordered yet

Non examples

  • A visitor who browsed the shop without creating an account

Relations

  • Customer has many Orders
  • Customer has many Subscriptions

Implemented in

models/marts/dim_customer.sql · customer_id
Never reviewed

Incoming relations

  • has subtype Active customer
  • has subtype Churned account (proposed)
  • has attribute Customer region
  • measured by Retention rate

Customer is selected: its depth-1 neighbourhood is at full contrast and everything else fades. Each concept is a box enclosing its name, with its kind glyph (colour and shape) inside. Every edge touching Customer is labelled with its relation name (and cardinality, where recorded); the hovered Customer → Order label is at full contrast. Faded edges show their label on hover. Badges: a warning on Active customer (illustrative drift) and an info finding on Subscription (related_to).

Customer

customer

Definition

A person or business that has created an account with the shop and accepted the terms of service, whether or not they have bought anything.

Don't call it

  • client — Finance and the old CRM say 'client'
  • shopper — Marketing copy

Examples

  • Someone who signed up yesterday and has not ordered yet

Not examples

  • A visitor who browsed the shop without creating an account

Relationships

  • Customer has many Orders
  • Customer has many Subscriptions
  • has subtype Active customer, Churned account (proposed)
  • has attribute Customer region
  • measured by Retention rate
